# IMPERIONX OS v4.3 — Universal Business Operations Dashboard

## Objective
Bring the existing business network and commerce workflows into a clearer operational cockpit without duplicating APIs or weakening tenant boundaries.

## Delivered
- Business command center with live counts for open purchase requests, quotations awaiting buyer decisions, active deals and completed deals.
- Pipeline lane counts for requests, submitted quotations, in-progress fulfilment and completed transactions.
- All / Needs attention / Active deals views across requests, quotes and transactions.
- Buyer controls to close an open purchase request; the backend closes submitted quotes with it.
- Buyer controls to reject submitted quotations and supplier controls to withdraw their own submitted quotations.
- Existing quote acceptance, transaction status, fulfilment and dispute workflows remain in place.
- Clear disclosure that the platform does not process payments, hold funds or guarantee supplier performance.

## Safety and scope
The dashboard uses existing authenticated tenant-scoped endpoints. It does not expose private contact data, bypass accepted-connection requirements, automate external communications, process payments or alter the trust/reputation model. The dashboard is an operational view, not a financial ledger; currencies remain separate.
